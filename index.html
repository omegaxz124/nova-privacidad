Privacy Policy for Nova Imaginum

This privacy policy applies to the application Nova Imaginum (hereinafter, "the Application"), developed by A.M.M.

1. Application Purpose and Data Processing

Nova Imaginum is a multimedia editing and file management tool that prioritizes your privacy.

  • Local Processing: All video, audio, image, and document files that you edit or manage with the Application are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, or store your personal files on any external server.
  • No Accounts or Registration: We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information to use the Application.
  • No Tracking or Ads: The Application does not include advertisements, does not use third-party tracking tools for advertising purposes, and does not sell your data to third parties.

2. Internet Connection

The Application operates offline. However, to enable the subtitle translation feature, the Application requires an Internet connection solely for the initial download of the corresponding language package. Once the package is downloaded, the translation feature will work offline. This download does not collect or transmit any of your personal data.

3. Device Permissions

To function properly, the Application may request access to your device's storage. This permission is used exclusively to read, edit, and save the multimedia files you select. We do not access files you have not chosen.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties. When you use the Application's "share" function, it invokes your Android system's native menu so you can choose which external application (such as WhatsApp or Telegram) to send the file to. We do not transmit data to those third parties on our own behalf.

5. Legal Compliance and User Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data, we do not process personal information for commercial purposes. However, you retain the following rights under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR in Europe and CCPA in California):

  • The right to access, correct, or delete any personal data (though we hold none).
  • The right to know that we do not sell or share your personal information.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. We recommend reviewing this page periodically to stay informed of any changes. Changes take effect immediately upon posting on this page.
